\q{1.}{An engineer who is studying the tensile strength of a steel alloy intended for use in golf club shafts knows that tensile strength is approximately normally distributed. A random sample of 12 specimens has a mean tensile strength of 3250 psi.
\begin{rom}\item Test the hypothesis that mean strength is 3500 psi. Use 1\% significance level. Standard deviation is known to be 60 psi.\item What is the smallest level of significance at which you would be willing to reject the null hypothesis in part (i)?\item Explain how you could answer the question in part (i) with a two-sided confidence interval on mean tensile strength.\item How will the test be performed if the standard deviation is not known?\end{rom}}{10}{3}{3}
\q{2.}{Inter arrival times in minutes between successive customers in a hotel is exponentially distributed with a mean of 2 minutes.
\begin{rom}\item Compute the probability that inter arrival time between two customers is at most 2 minutes.\item What is the probability that at least 2 customers arrive in one minute?\item What is the mean-time till every 3\textsuperscript{rd} customer arrives?\item What is the probability of no customers arriving in a span of 3 minutes?\end{rom}}{10}{3}{3}
\q{3.}{\begin{rom}\item What are the hypothesis statements and the statistic to be used in a two-sided test of variance of a population? \q{4.}{For the sample (5, 2, 3, 1, 0, 1, 3, 3) from a population,
\begin{rom}\item Construct a 90\% confidence interval on population mean if the population standard deviation is known to be 2.\item What should the sample size be in order to keep the width of the confidence interval to less than 2?\item Construct a 95\% confidence interval on population mean if the population variance is unknown.\end{rom}}{10}{3}{4}
\q{5.}{An electrical firm manufactures light bulbs that have a life, before burn-out, that is normally distributed with mean equal to 800 hours and a standard deviation of 40 hours. Find
